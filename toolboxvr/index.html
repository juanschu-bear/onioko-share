01Inside ToolboxVR

3 ways it could show up in your worlds.

Practice conversations

A trainee practices a customer conversation with one of your characters. The character could notice when her pauses get longer and give her room. Afterwards, the trainer could read what was observed, in careful language: “could”, never “is”.

Progress over weeks

Next session, the character could remember where she got stuck and pick up right there. Her progress would be measured only against herself, never against the class.

Onboarding and guided tours

A guide character could remember what each new hire asked last week and build on it, so the second visit doesn't start from zero.

These are examples. Where it fits best in your worlds, you know better than we do.

03EU AI Act

Signals, never emotion labels.

Article 5(1)(f) of the EU AI Act is about workplaces and education institutions, the settings vocational and corporate training take place in. Our technology doesn't guess what a learner feels. It measures signals, finds patterns, and leaves the meaning to a person.

Article 5(1)(f) prohibits AI systems “to infer emotions of a natural person in the areas of workplace and education institutions”, except for medical or safety reasons.
